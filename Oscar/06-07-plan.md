## The question

Using only the **first 3 months** of behaviour (Apr–Jun 2005, columns
numbered 4–6) plus the customer's details, predict whether they default in
October 2005. Agreed with Alex after his review
([`Alex/01-review-and-next-steps.md`](../Alex/01-review-and-next-steps.md)).
September's status (`PAY_1`) comes too late to act on.

**Baseline to beat** (Alex, plain logistic regression, Apr–Jun only):
AUC 0.652, Brier 0.160 against 0.172 for predicting 22% for everyone
(skill ≈ 0.07). It catches 101 of 1,658 defaulters at threshold 0.5.
